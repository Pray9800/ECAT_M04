Chapter5 Various Operation Methods
( 3 ) Servo On (automatic servo on is enabled)
Servo-ON is performed to excite the motor and make it ready for operation, and conversely,
Servo-OFF is performed to de-energize the motor. The following shows the case where
servo-ON is automatically executed when the control power is turned on (0516H Servo state
at power-on: 0 Servo-ON).
①Turn on the main power supply and control power supply.
②The motor is excited, the READY output (ready for operation) is turned on, and the SV-
STS output (servo on state) is turned on. ※1 ※2 ※3
The electromagnetic brake is in the release state. ※5
③Make sure that the SV-STS output (servo on state) is ON, and turn on the SV-OFF input
(servo off). ※1
READY output turns OFF. The motor enters the de-energized state and the SV-STS output
turns OFF. The electromagnetic brake enters the holding state. ※5
④Confirm that the SV-STS output is OFF, and turn the SV-OFF input to OFF. ※1 ※4
The READY output is turned on.
⑤Make sure that the READY output is ON and the SV-STS output is OFF, and turn on the
SV-ON input (servo on). ※1
The motor is excited and the SV-STS output is turned on. The electromagnetic brake is
in the release state. ※5
⑥Confirm that the SV-STS output is ON and turn off the SV-ON input. ※1 ※4
