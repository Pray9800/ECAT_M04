10. Troubleshooting
Troubleshooting describes how to respond to alarms, information, and other abnormalities of the
monitoring system.
When an alarm occurs, the motor stops to protect the driver. Information helps maintain the
equipment by confirming the condition before the alarm occurs.
10.1 Alarm
When an alarm occurs, the motor stops and the alarm LED blinks.
The number of times the LED blinks, various communication I/Fs, control outputs, and dedicated
applications can be used to check the type of alarm that is occurring.
When an alarm occurs, the ALM LED blinks the alarm number at 1-second intervals and goes off
for 2 seconds.
( 1 ) Disarming alarms
Once the cause of the alarm has been eliminated and safety is assured, clear the alarm by one
of the following methods
- Alarm clear from network communication
- Alarm clear from control signal
- Alarm clear from dedicated applications
- Power back on
(Caution)
Some alarms cannot be cleared except by turning the power back on. Please refer to the list
of alarms below.
