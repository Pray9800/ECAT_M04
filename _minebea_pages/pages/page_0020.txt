Chapter3 Installation and Wiring
3.2 Wiring
Caution
 Turn off the power and make sure that the indicator is off before disconnecting or
inserting the connector. Residual voltage may cause electric shock.
 When inserting the connector, hold the connector body and insert it straight and
securely. Incomplete connection may cause damage to the motor or motor controller.
 When unplugging the connector, pull it straight out while releasing the lock. Pulling
out the connector by holding the cable may cause damage to the connector.
3.2.1 Power supply connection
Connect the main power supply, control power supply, ground wire, and electromagnetic brake to
the motor controller using the terminal block for power input terminals provided.
