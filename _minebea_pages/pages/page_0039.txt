Chapter3 Installation and Wiring
If the torque off function is not used, connect the three jumper wires as shown in the table
below.
The connectors in the accessories are pre-connected with jumpers.
No. Connection point
1 +14V (5-pin) - TO1+ (11-pin)
2 TO1- (4-pin) - TO2+ (10-pin)
3 TO2- (3-pin) - GND (9-pin)
( 2 ) Connection of control cables
Caution
 Do not route control cables in the same piping as other cables. Noise may cause
malfunction.
 The cable length between the motor controller and the PLC/controller should not exceed
3 m.
1. Peel off about 7 to 8 mm of the lead wire sheath.
Matching lead wires are AWG 26 to 18. Please provide your own lead wires.
2. Check the pin assignment, and while pressing the orange button with a flat-blade screwdriver
(①), connect the lead wires to the supplied TO/EDM terminal connector (②).
