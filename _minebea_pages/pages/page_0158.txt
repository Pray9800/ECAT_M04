Chapter9 Internal register
Homing operation setting 8: LS- operation
This is the mode to perform homing by LS-. The edge of LS- is detected.
● When the homing start position is LS-undetected
High = Inactive, Low = Active (at default)
No. operation
① The edge of LS- is detected in the - direction at the homing speed.
② The edge of LS- is detected in the + direction at the homing search speed.
③ The edge of LS- is detected in the - direction at the homing search speed, and the
detection operation ends.
● When the homing start position is LS-detected
High = Inactive, Low = Active (at default)
No. operation
① The edge of LS- is detected in the + direction at the homing search speed.
② The edge of LS- is detected in the - direction at the homing search speed, and the
detection operation ends.
