Chapter7 Torque off function
7.3 Operation
7.3.1 Transition from Normal to Torque off
Torque off request TO1 and TO2 are used to shut off the power supply to the motor.
For details of wiring and signals, see ➡ 3.2.7 Torque off request/EDM diagnostics controller
connection.
(1) Torque off request TO1 and TO2 are turned from OFF to ON.
(2) The motor will be in a state of no excitation, and the unit will be in a torque-off state.
The control signal TO-MON output is turned on, the READY output (ready for operation) is
turned off, and the SV-STS output (servo on state) is turned off. ※1
The EDM monitoring signal EDM1 and EDM2 outputs are turned from OFF to ON. ※2
The PWR LED (green) goes from solid to blinking.
The electromagnetic brake is in a holding state. ※3
