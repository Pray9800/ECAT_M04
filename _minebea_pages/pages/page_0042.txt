5. Various Operation Methods
There are three ways to operate the motor controller: PLC operation, operation by dedicated
application, and operation by communication network.
Operation Method Contents
Operation by communication Operate according to communication specifications from the
network communication network. See communication Networks chapter(➡8 RS485
Communication).
PLC operation I/O signals and pulse commands are sent from the I/O unit or
positioning unit of the PLC for operation.
Operation by dedicated The computer and the motor controller are connected via USB and
application operated using a dedicated application.
