3. Installation and Wiring
3.1 Installation
( 1 ) Location
The motor controller is designed and manufactured for equipment integration. Install in the
following locations that are well ventilated and easily accessible for inspection.
 Inside an enclosure installed indoors (but with ventilation openings)
 Ambient operating temperature 0 to +50°C (not frozen)
 Ambient operating humidity: 85% or less (no condensation)
 Where it is not exposed to direct sunlight
 Where there is no dust, iron powder, etc.
 Where there is less salt.
 No explosive atmosphere, no harmful gases, and no liquids.
 Locations where water (rain or water droplets), oil (oil droplets), and other liquids
will not splash on it.
 Where continuous vibration or excessive shock is not applied.
 Where electromagnetic noise (welding machines, power equipment, etc.) is low
 Where there is no radioactive material or magnetic field and no vacuum
 Less than 1,000 m above sea level
