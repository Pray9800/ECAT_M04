Chapter3 Installation and Wiring
( 2 ) Installation of motor controller
When installing a motor controller in a control panel or other equipment, give full
consideration to operability, maintainability, and environmental resistance.
 Do not install equipment with high heat generation or electromagnetic noise in the
vicinity.
 If the ambient temperature exceeds 50°C, ventilate the room with a fan to keep the
temperature below 50°C.
 Be sure to install vertically.
 Mount on 32 mm or 35 mm wide DIN rails.
 Install at least 20 mm away from other equipment horizontally and vertically.
 Two or more motor controllers can be installed in close proximity to each other in
the horizontal direction.
