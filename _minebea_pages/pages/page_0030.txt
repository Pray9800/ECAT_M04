Chapter3 Installation and Wiring
( 2 ) Connection of control cables
Caution
 Do not route control cables in the same piping as other cables. Noise may cause
malfunction.
 The cable length between the motor controller and the PLC/controller should not exceed
3 m.
1. Peel off about 7 to 8 mm of the lead wire sheath.
Matching lead wires are AWG 30 to 20. Please provide your own lead wires.
2. Check the pin assignment, and while pressing the orange button with a flat-blade screwdriver
(①), connect the lead wire to the supplied connector for the control signal terminal (②).
