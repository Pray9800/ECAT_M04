7.3.2 Return from Torque off to Normal
Torque off request TO1 and TO2 are used to cut off the power supply to the motor.
For details of wiring and signals, see ➡ 3.2.7 Torque off request/EDM diagnostics controller
connection.
(1) Set the torque off request TO1 and TO2 from OFF to ON.
The EDM monitoring signal EDM1 and EDM2 outputs are turned off. ※2
(2) Confirm that the EDM monitoring signal EDM1 and EDM2 outputs are OFF, and wait for more
than the time set by the parameter 0B01H (torque off state clear invalid time). ※2
(3) Turn the TO-CLR input from ON to OFF. If the TO-CLR input is OFF, turn it on and then
turn it off again.
The torque off state is released and the system returns to its normal state.
The TO-MON output is turned OFF and the READY output is turned on. ※3
The PWR LED (green) will go from blinking to solid.
(4) Confirm that the READY output is ON and the SV-STS output is OFF, and turn on the SV-ON
input (servo on). ※1
The motor is excited and the SV-STS output is turned on. ※3
The electromagnetic brake is in the release state. ※4
(5) Confirm that the SV-STS output is ON and turn off the SV-ON input. ※4
