Chapter3 Installation and Wiring
( 2 ) Connect power cable/ground wire/brake cable
Caution
 Do not route the power cable in the same piping as other cables. Noise may cause
malfunction.
1. Peel off about 8 mm of the lead wire sheath.
Matching lead wires are AWG 24 to 16. Please provide your own lead wires.
2. Check the pin assignment, and while pressing the orange button with a flat-blade
screwdriver(①), connect the lead wires to the terminal block for the power input terminal
provided(②).
3. Insert the terminal block for the power input terminal into the power input terminal (CN4)
of the motor controller.
