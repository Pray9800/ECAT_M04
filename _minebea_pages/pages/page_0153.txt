Chapter9 Internal register
Homing operation setting 3: LS+ operation
This is the mode to perform homing using LS+.
● Detects the edge of LS+. When LS+ is not detected at the homing start position
High = Inactive, Low = Active (at default)
No. operation
① The edge of LS+ is detected in the positive direction at the homing speed.
② The edge of LS+ is detected in the negative direction at the homing search speed.
③ The edge of LS+ is detected in the positive direction at the homing search speed, and
the detection operation ends.
● When the homing start position is LS+ detected
High = Inactive, Low = Active (at default)
No. operation
① The edge of LS+ is detected in the negative direction at the homing search speed.
② The edge of LS+ is detected in the positive direction at the homing search speed, and
the detection operation ends.
