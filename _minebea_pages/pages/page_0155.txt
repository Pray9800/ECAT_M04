Chapter9 Internal register
Homing operation setting 5: LS+, Z operation
This mode performs homing using LS+ and Z phase.
After detecting the LS+ edge, the rising edge of the Z phase on the negative side is
detected.
● When the homing start position is LS+ not detected
High = Inactive, Low = Active (at default)
No. operation
① The edge of LS+ is detected in the positive direction at the homing speed.
② The edge of LS+ is detected in the negative direction at the homing search speed.
③ LS+ edge detection is performed in the + direction at the homing search speed.
④ The Z edge is detected in the negative direction at the homing search speed, and the
detection operation ends.
● When the homing start position is LS+ detected
High = Inactive, Low = Active (at default)
No. operation
① The edge of LS+ is detected in the negative direction at the homing search speed.
② LS+ edge detection is performed in the + direction at the homing search speed.
③ The Z edge is detected in the negative direction at the homing search speed, and the
detection operation ends.
