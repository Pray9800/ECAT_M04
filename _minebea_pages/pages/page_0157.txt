Chapter9 Internal register
Homing operation setting 7: ORG (- side), Z operation
This mode performs homing using ORG and Z phase.
After detecting the negative edge of ORG, the rising edge of the positive Z phase is
detected.
● When the homing start position is on the negative side of ORG
High = Inactive, Low = Active (at default)
No. operation
① The edge of ORG is detected in the positive direction at the homing speed.
② The edge of ORG is detected in the - direction at the homing speed.
③ The edge of ORG is detected in the positive direction at the homing search
speed.
④ The Z-phase edge is detected in the positive direction at the homing search
speed, and the detection operation ends.
● When the homing start position is on the positive side of ORG
High = Inactive, Low = Active (at default)
No. operation
① LS+ edge detection is performed in the + direction at the homing speed.
② The edge of ORG is detected in the - direction at the homing speed.
③ The edge of ORG is detected in the positive direction at the homing speed.
④ The edge of ORG is detected in the - direction at the homing speed.
⑤ The edge of ORG is detected in the positive direction at the homing search
speed.
⑥ The Z-phase edge is detected in the positive direction at the homing search
speed, and the detection operation ends.
