Chapter9 Internal register
Homing operation setting 1: ORG (+ side) operation
This mode performs homing using ORG (origin signal).
Detects the positive edge of ORG.
● When the homing start position is on the positive side of ORG
High = Inactive, Low = Active (at default)
No. operation
① The edge of ORG is detected in the - direction at the homing speed.
② The edge of ORG is detected in the positive direction at the homing speed.
③ The edge of ORG is detected in the - direction at the homing search speed, and
the detection operation ends.
● When the homing start position is on the negative side of ORG
High = Inactive, Low = Active (at default)
No. operation
① LS- edge detection is performed in the - direction at the homing speed.
② The edge of ORG is detected in the positive direction at the homing speed.
③ The edge of ORG is detected in the - direction at the homing speed.
④ The edge of ORG is detected in the positive direction at the homing speed.
⑤ The edge of ORG is detected in the - direction at the homing search speed, and
the detection operation ends.
