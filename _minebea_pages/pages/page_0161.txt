Chapter9 Internal register
Homing operation setting 11: + direction pressing operation
This mode uses torque limit to perform pressing.
Detects the torque limit in the + direction.
High = Inactive, Low = Active (at default)
No. operation
① The torque limit is detected in the positive direction at the homing speed, and
the detection operation ends.
② The homing offset (0902H) moves in the opposite direction to the detection.
Homing operation setting 12: - direction pressing operation
This mode uses torque limit to perform pressing.
Detects the torque limit in the - direction.
High = Inactive, Low = Active (at default)
No. operation
① The torque limit is detected in the negative direction at the homing speed, and
the detection operation ends.
② The homing offset (0902H) moves in the opposite direction to the detection.
