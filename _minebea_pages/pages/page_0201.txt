11. Maintenance and inspection
11.1 Inspection
For safe use of motor controller and motors with encoders, we recommend periodic inspections.
( 1 ) checklist
 Scratched or stressed motor and encoder cables, disconnected connectors
 Loose motor mounting screws, etc.
 Smooth operation of motor
11.2 Disposal
When disposing of the product, please dispose of it properly as industrial waste.
