Chapter5 Various Operation Methods
5.4 PLC operation
The PLC is connected to the control signal terminal (CN1) of the motor controller, and operation
is performed by commands from the PLC. Parameters and programs must be set in advance via RS485
communication or dedicated application.
For details on how to access internal registers via RS485 communication, refer to ➡8 RS485
Communication and ➡ 9 Internal register.
For details on how to use the dedicated application, please refer to ➡ 5.5 Operation by
dedicated application.
