Chapter3 Installation and Wiring
3.2.3 Encoder Connection
Use the encoder cable to connect the encoder to the motor controller.
Please use our cable for the encoder. If you make your own cable, we recommend using a shielded
cable and grounding the shield.
( 1 ) Connecting the encoder cable
Caution
 Do not route the encoder cable in the same piping as other cables. Noise may cause
malfunction.
 Do not apply excessive stress to the connectors. Doing so may cause fire, electric
shock, or damage to the unit (circuit board).
 When attaching a motor to a moving part, use a separate cable for the moving part that
has a superior number of bends and bending radius.
 The recommended length of the encoder cable is 5 m or less; exceeding 5 m may cause an
increase in radiated noise from the unit.
1. Connect the motor controller to the encoder with the supplied encoder cable.
The connector on the motor controller side is plugged into the encoder terminal (CN2).
After connecting, make sure the connector is locked.
To remove the connector, pull it out while pressing down on the connector claws.
