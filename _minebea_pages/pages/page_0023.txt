Chapter3 Installation and Wiring
3.2.2 Connection of motor with encoder
Use a motor cable to connect the motor with encoder to the motor controller.
Please use our own cables for the motor. If you make your own cable, we recommend using a
shielded cable and grounding the shield.
( 1 ) Connecting the cable for the motor
Caution
 Do not route the cable for the motor in the same piping as other cables. Noise may
cause malfunction.
 Do not apply excessive stress to the connectors. Doing so may cause fire, electric
shock, or damage to the unit (circuit board).
 When attaching a motor to a moving part, use a separate cable for the moving part that
has a superior number of bends and bending radius.
 The recommended length of the motor cable is 5 m or less.
1. The motor cable connects the motor controller and the motor with encoder.
The connector on the motor controller side is inserted into the motor terminal (CN3). After
connecting, make sure the connector is locked.
To remove the connector, pull it out while pressing down on the connector claws.
