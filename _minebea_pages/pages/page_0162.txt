Chapter9 Internal register
Homing operation setting 13: + direction pressing, Z operation
This mode performs a push using the torque limit, and then performs a return to the
origin using the Z phase.
After detecting the torque limit in the + direction, the + edge of the Z phase is
detected.
High = Inactive, Low = Active (at default)
No. operation
① The torque limit is detected in the positive direction at the homing speed.
② The edge of the Z phase is detected in the - direction at the homing speed.
③ The Z-phase edge is detected in the positive direction at the homing search
speed.
④ The edge of the Z phase is detected in the - direction at the homing search
speed, and the detection operation ends.
Homing operation setting 14: - direction pressing, Z operation
This mode performs a push using the torque limit, and then performs a return to the
origin using the Z phase.
After detecting the torque limit in the + direction, the + edge of the Z phase is
detected.
High = Inactive, Low = Active (at default)
No. operation
① The torque limit is detected in the negative direction at the homing speed.
② The edge of the Z phase is detected in the positive direction at the homing
speed.
③ The edge of the Z phase is detected in the - direction at the homing speed.
④ The Z-phase edge is detected in the positive direction at the homing search
speed.
