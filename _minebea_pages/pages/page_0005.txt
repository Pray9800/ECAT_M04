Precautions for use
This section describes precautions for using the product.
• Do not perform insulation resistance measurements or dielectric strength tests on the
encoder, as this may damage the unit.
• Do not perform a dielectric strength test. Also, when measuring the insulation
resistance, be sure to separate the motor from the unit, as this may damage the unit.
• This unit uses semiconductor devices, so please handle it with care to prevent damage
caused by static electricity.
• To protect the encoder, keep the motor surface temperature below 80°C.
• There are screw holes on the side of this device, but do not screw anything into them or
insert foreign objects. Doing so may damage the device.
• Ensure the motor does not enter a freewheeling state during power outages or similar
events. Doing so may damage the device due to voltage surges caused by regeneration. If
this cannot be avoided, operate within the load inertia ratio and motor speed ranges
specified in the table below.
Motor Use range
M23KMH744CSMA00 Inertia ratio up to 20 times: Motor speed up to 2400 rpm
M23KMK358CSMA00 Inertia ratio up to 20 times: Motor speed up to 3000 rpm
M23KMH041CSMA00 There are no restrictions on the inertia ratio or motor speed.
