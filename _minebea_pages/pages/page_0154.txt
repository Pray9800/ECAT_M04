Homing operation setting 4: Z (+ side) operation
This mode performs homing using the Z phase.
Detects the positive edge of the Z phase.
● When the homing start position is on the positive side of the Z phase
High = Inactive, Low = Active (at default)
No. operation
① The edge of the Z phase is detected in the - direction at the homing speed.
② The edge of the Z phase is detected in the positive direction at the homing
search speed.
③ The edge of the Z phase is detected in the - direction at the homing search
speed, and the detection operation ends.
● When the homing start position is on the negative side of the Z phase
High = Inactive, Low = Active (at default)
No. operation
① LS- edge detection is performed in the - direction at the homing speed.
② The edge of the Z phase is detected in the positive direction at the homing
speed.
③ The edge of the Z phase is detected in the - direction at the homing speed.
④ The Z-phase edge is detected in the positive direction at the homing search
speed.
⑤ The edge of the Z phase is detected in the - direction at the homing search
speed, and the detection operation ends.
