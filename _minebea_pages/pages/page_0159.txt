Chapter9 Internal register
Homing operation setting 9: Z (- side) operation
This mode performs homing using the Z phase.
Detects the negative edge of the Z phase.
● When the homing start position is on the negative side of the Z phase
High = Inactive, Low = Active (at default)
No. operation
① The edge of the Z phase is detected in the positive direction at the homing speed.
② The Z-phase edge is detected in the negative direction at the homing search speed.
③ The Z-phase edge is detected in the positive direction at the homing search speed, and
the detection operation ends.
● When the homing start position is on the positive side of the Z phase
High = Inactive, Low = Active (at default)
No. operation
① The edge of LS+ is detected in the positive direction at the homing speed.
② The Z-phase edge is detected in the negative direction at the homing speed.
③ The edge of the Z phase is detected in the positive direction at the homing speed.
④ The Z-phase edge is detected in the negative direction at the homing search speed.
⑤ The Z-phase edge is detected in the positive direction at the homing search speed, and
the detection operation ends.
