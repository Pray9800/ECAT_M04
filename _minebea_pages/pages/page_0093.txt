Chapter8 RS485 Communication
8.1.2 Connecting RS485 Communication Cables
Note
 Do not route the RS485 communication cable in the same pipe as any other cable. Noise
may cause malfunction.
 The cable length for RS485 communication is as follows:30It should be less than m.
1. Peel off about 9 mm of the lead wire sheath.
Matching lead wires are AWG 28～16. Please provide your own lead wires.
2. Check the pin assignment, press the white button with a flat-blade screwdriver ((1)),
and connect the lead wires to the RS485 communication terminal (CN6 or C)N7) ((2)).
It does not matter whether you connect to CN6 or CN7. The other spare communication
terminal can be used to connect to other motor controllers..
