Chapter8 RS485 Communication
8.1.3 Communication settings
An RS485 device is connected to the RS485 communication terminals (CN6, CN7) of the motor
controller, and operation is performed by commands from the RS485 device. (Parameters and
programs must be set in advance via RS485 communication or dedicated application..
For more information on how to access internal registers in RS485 communication, see ➡8
RS485 Communication and ➡ 9 Internal register.
How to operate in the dedicated application, see ➡ 5.5 Operation by dedicated application.
8.1.4 Switch Settings
 If the motor controller is installed at the end of the RS485 communication, the
configuration switch (SW1AND SW2) to ON (RS485 termination resistor ON). It is
factory set to "OFF" (RS485 termination resistor OFF).
