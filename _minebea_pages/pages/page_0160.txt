Chapter9 Internal register
Homing operation setting 10: LS-, Z operation
This mode performs homing using LS- and Z phase.
After detecting the LS- edge, the rising edge of the Z phase on the + side is detected.
● When the homing start position is LS-undetected
High = Inactive, Low = Active (at default)
No. operation
① The edge of LS- is detected in the - direction at the homing speed.
② The edge of LS- is detected in the + direction at the homing search speed.
③ The edge of LS- is detected in the - direction at the homing search speed.
④ The Z edge is detected in the + direction at the homing search speed, and the detection
operation ends.
● When the homing start position is LS-detected
High = Inactive, Low = Active (at default)
No. operation
① The edge of LS- is detected in the + direction at the homing search speed.
② The edge of LS- is detected in the - direction at the homing search speed.
③ The Z edge is detected in the + direction at the homing search speed, and the detection
operation ends.
