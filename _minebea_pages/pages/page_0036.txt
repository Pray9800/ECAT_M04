Chapter3 Installation and Wiring
3.2.5 USB device connection
Connect the PC to the motor controller using a USB cable.
Caution
 Do not route the USB cable in the same conduit as other cables. Noise may cause
malfunction.
 The length of the USB cable should be less than 3m.
1. Plug the USB cable into the USB communication terminal of the motor controller.
Please prepare USB cable (A-miniB type) by yourself.
