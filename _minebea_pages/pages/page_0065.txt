( 4 ) Servo On (Automatic Servo On is disabled)
Servo-ON is executed to make the motor excited and ready for operation, and conversely,
Servo-OFF is executed to make the motor de-energized. The following shows the case where
servo-ON is not executed automatically when the control power is turned on (0516H Servo
state at power-on: 1 Servo-OFF).
①Turn on the main power supply and control power supply.
②The READY output (ready for operation) is turned on. ※1 ※2
The motor is in a non-excitation state. The electromagnetic brake is held and maintained.
※5
③Confirm that the READY output is ON and the SV-STS output (servo on state) is OFF, and
then turn on the SV-ON input (servo on). When the servo is performed for the first time
after the power is turned on, the initialization operation is automatically executed.
※3
The READY output is turned off once.
After the initialization operation is complete, SV-STS output is ON、READY output is
ONand the motor is in an excited state. ※1
The electromagnetic brake is in the release state. ※5
④Confirm that the SV-STS output is ON and turn off the SV-ON input. ※4
⑤Make sure that the SV-STS output is ON, and turn on the SV-OFF input (servo off).The
READY output is turned off, the SV-STS output is turned off, and the motor is in a non-
excitation state. ※1
The electromagnetic brake is in a holding state. ※5
⑥Confirm that the SV-STS output is OFF and turn off the SV-OFF input. ※4
The READY output is turned on.
⑦Confirm that the READY output is ON and the SV-STS output is OFF, and turn on the SV-
ON input. The SV-STS output is turned on and the motor is in an excited state. ※1
The electromagnetic brake is in the release state. ※5
⑧Confirm that the SV-STS output is ON and turn off the SV-ON input. ※4
